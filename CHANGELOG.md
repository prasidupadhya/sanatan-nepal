# Changelog

## v0.1.0

- Five-branch knowledge graph with animated cross-topic relationships and deep-linkable reading pages.
- Four Vedas, Upanishadic introduction, core philosophy, eighteen Mahapuranas and practice/glossary content.
- Kuru and Ikshvaku/Raghu family explorers, epic story modes, eighteen Parvas and seven Kandas.
- Eighteen-chapter Gita explorer with original summaries and verse paraphrases.
- Nepal-focused Pashupatinath, Kumari, Newar, Karunamaya, Panchayan and historical material.
- Seven-festival rotating year-wheel and five-temple Leaflet map.
- Section quizzes, fuzzy search, bookmarks, reading completion and local score storage.
- Light, Dark and Diya themes, reduced-motion handling, keyboard graph controls and mobile layouts.
- Seven component/content tests and six production-browser tests, including fifteen automated page/theme accessibility scans.
- Owner-requested main-only workflow, fifteen-commit release history and no remaining feature branches.
